**Description:**

> No agent, no plugin, no account, no key. In regular Chrome, Awesome Open*'s Search
> panel offers Chrome's on-device model. You download it once, ask for two video
> editors that run in the browser, and the model calls the page's tools (find,
> arrange, compare) right in the page. The answer links to both in the Cabinet.
>
> Chrome's on-device model needs desktop Chrome 148 or later, 22 GB free disk space,
> and more than 4 GB of VRAM, or 16 GB of RAM and 4 CPU cores (Chrome's Prompt API
> documentation, as recorded on 2026-11-06).
>
> Recorded 2026-11-16 in Chrome 157 with an empty profile and no feature flags,
> from two takes: the model download on WebMCPlet 0.2.1, the question on 0.2.3; the
> switch is named on screen. Sped-up stretches are tagged with the real time.
