# V1: on our list

**Article claim:** "No tool installation: the URL itself provides the MCP." V1
premieres under "Example 2: How an Awesome List works with tools".

**Shows:** a person gives ChatGPT only the list's URL and a request. ChatGPT opens
Awesome Open\* in its cloud browser, uses the page's WebMCP tools (the page logs
each call), returns two video editors with their sources, and links to the
comparison, which opens on our site.

- **Client:** ChatGPT web, GPT-6 Astra Medium, Work mode, cloud browser enlarged.
- **Prompt:** "Open https://awesomeopen.github.io/awesome-open/ and use its tools to
  find me two self-hosted video editors. Compare them."
- **Footage:** take 3, 2026-11-16, SDR. ChatGPT reported "Worked for 1m 32s".
- **Length:** about 36 s from 2:22 of real time. Speed-ups are tagged; the cut
  where the owner had control of the cloud browser (0:58 to 1:38) is named on
  screen.
- **Values:** stars, licence and last push from the catalog on the shoot date. Each
  of ChatGPT's claims carries its source: catalog (checked against the record),
  web (as ChatGPT cited it) or generated (ChatGPT's own pick).
- **Captions:** English, burned in and as `.vtt`; no voice-over.
- **Not in this cut:** the code reveal (extended cut) and the family-photo
  no-match.

**Open for gate 1:** wording of each caption, the zoom framing, and whether the
