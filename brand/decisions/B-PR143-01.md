# Body-led jar fitting with physical-wall clipping

[PR #143](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/pull/143) changes the
complete-silhouette fitting requirement in [B-PR131-01](B-PR131-01.md) and
[B-PR127-01](B-PR127-01.md). Their source preparation, selected-project identity,
weekly fairness and unchanged vessel/interaction contracts remain. The seven
vessels retain the order in [B-PR134-02](B-PR134-02.md).

The owner explicitly permits peripheral protrusions to disappear at the inner
glass edge, where they can read as pressed against the vessel. This does not
permit stretching, changing the original drawing, reducing its opacity or
cutting it at a floating inset rectangle. Complete illustrations remain in
record views and the Spotlight archive.

Historical source confirms that the commissioning OpenObserve specimen used
300% canvas width with overflow clipped before the weekly draw activated on
October 19. Its complete subject was about 154px tall at the fixed 310×207px
vessel footprint. Whole-silhouette fitting reduced that to about 83px, then 64px
with the corrected inner walls. The original and cut-out bytes are unchanged.
The new source-hash-bound horizontal span fits the mineral column and preserves
complete height, restoring approximately 154px. Left ribbons and forked foot
tips can disappear behind the actual wall; no pixels are erased from the asset.

Manually traced source-photograph contours follow inner necks, shoulders, side
walls, curved floors and the low jar's leaning-lid occlusion. Their mask stays
fixed relative to the vessel while the existing specimen depth and idle layers
move. Fitting boxes and clipping contours are independent: the old low-lid x66%
rectangle cannot clip an upper-body protrusion before it reaches visible glass.
All seven photographic sources were inspected at full size before calibration.

Fit body width to the interior without a horizontal motion inset, preserving
aspect ratio and 2.25px vertical idle clearance. Put spare height above the
subject. Source-matched body bounds drive assignment and placement consistently;
missing, stale or invalid body metadata falls back to complete-subject fitting.
Try all 5,040 assignments, protecting worst occupancy before total fill. The
saved seven, draw counts and selection probabilities never change. Placement
among the fixed vessels can change to improve body fit.

This changes display composition only. Original photographs and illustrations,
cut-outs, responsive derivatives, opacity, cork/stickers, glass-light/crack
geometry, vessel dimensions, motion and third-click/Inspect behavior remain.
No asset or skill checksum refresh is required.

Validation requires actual matched base/head screenshots plus desktop/mobile,
current occupants, representative tall/wide artwork, motion and reduced-motion
states. The outlier route is CI-only and never updates draw evidence. Assertions
check body scaling, source guards, fixed wall masks, existing movement allowance
and unchanged selection. Passing them alone is not visual acceptance. Initial
preview inspection and final exact-head CI results are recorded in the PR.
