# Default display approval for new specimen artwork

The owner directed on 2026-10-17 that future images be approved by default,
with individual drawings redone later if needed. This record captures that
standing direction without publishing the private conversation or claiming
that the owner individually reviewed each image. The documentation change is
submitted in [PR #109](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/pull/109);
accepted describes the owner's policy decision, not a merged implementation.

For new specimen artwork, use the existing `approved` manifest status after
actual pixel inspection and technical, provenance, rights and safety checks.
Record the real reviewer (including AI attribution), actual findings and the
owner-standing-direction basis in the manifest. Aesthetic/style concerns remain
visible as nonblocking review notes rather than withholding the image.

Missing checks, unsafe or invalid assets, identity mismatches, unresolved rights
or provenance, and official-logo misrepresentation are not aesthetic exceptions.
Pending and rejected states remain available for incomplete checks and confirmed
blocking issues. Do not fabricate human review or a clean visual assessment.

The [specimen handoff](../../site/SPECIMEN-ART.md#default-display-approval-and-review)
and [illustration reference](../illustration.md#deliver-and-review) define the
operating rule. The existing approved-only render gate remains intact; this is
a documentation-only clarification of how future records receive that status.
Draft PR review, validation and separate owner publication approval remain.
There is no automatic merge, deployment or repository-protection change.

Existing artwork bytes and historical statuses are untouched, and existing
accepted images are not regenerated without a request. No asset role, checksum
inventory, pinned skill, automation or code changes are part of this PR.
The remaining specimen concept and optional-artwork behavior in B03 and B29
remain unchanged; this record does not fully supersede either decision.
