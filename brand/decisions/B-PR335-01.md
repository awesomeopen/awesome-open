# Simpler cabinet and a captioned Search corner button

Owner review of the live Catalog on 2026-11-15, after #333 and #334 were merged.

**Cabinet.** The opened cabinet shows, under its heading, the search field, its results,
the selected projects as outlined names with ×, a Compare button and the evidence table.
The numbered steps, the Arrange step with Earlier and Later, the introduction paragraph,
the "Completed" status and the human Cancel button are removed; agents still set the order
with `cabinet_arrange` and cancel through their own signal. The table's top-left cell is
empty. Each project name in the table carries ×, which takes the project out of the
selection; its column stays, marked ↻, and ↻ compares again without it. Copy URL reads
"Copied" for two seconds. "Agents, activity & method" opens with "Native WebMCP: N tools
available to your agent or in-page assistant", where N counts the tools registered on the
page (seven on the Catalog: four cabinet tools and WebMCPlet's three).

**Corner buttons.** The cabinet button is compressed to 40px. WebMCPlet's button takes the
same layout in Prussian: the search loupe in the darker square, "Search" over a smaller
"WebMCPlet". The page styles it through the parts WebMCPlet 0.2.0 exposes. The dialog's
footer, in small text, says the search is a WebMCPlet showcase, what WebMCPlet does, offers
"Hide the Search button" as a link and names `data-webmcplet-panel="off"` for list admins.
This changes the corner button's label in [B-PR304-01](B-PR304-01.md) and
[B-PR333-01](B-PR333-01.md); the stacking order is unchanged.

**List head and plate.** The wordmark is centred over the list. The search field has no
placeholder, an `--app-line` border and a 2em radius. The plate's specimen label has 2px
padding.

**Phone.** About and its riders cover the screen, so the About head carries × to return to
the list. Back to an address without a plate or sheet also returns to the list.

**Pane.** A plate's links to Observatory, Scoreboard or Spotlight, such as its Trophies
line, open that page in the pane.

Rationale: the owner found the three steps too involved for comparing at most six
projects, Earlier and Later closed the cabinet (a re-rendered button counted as a click
outside it), "Search this list" read as a second copy of the Catalog search, the tool count
said four where the browser listed seven, and Copy URL gave no visible response.

× and ↻ are typographic characters, listed beside ↗ and ↺ in the [icon map](../icons.md).
