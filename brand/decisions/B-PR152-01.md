# Evidence meanings are stated positively

The owner asked to remove "this is not X" phrasing from the repository. An
inventory of the tracked text found the evidence contract repeated as slogans
("stars are not quality", "unknown is not negative", "alternatives are not
ancestry") in `AGENTS.md`, `PRODUCT.md`, the project guide, the icon map and the
design reference. Agents copy that sentence shape into docs, comments and copy.

The [evidence contract](../foundation.md#evidence-contract) keeps each label's
meaning and moves the excluded readings to a review-only column. Other files link
to it instead of restating it. The project guide's writing style adds a
positive-statement rule: say what a value, label or rule is and link its method;
state a limit that matters once, in the method section or the "(i)" disclosure.
Instructions and constraints keep their "do not" and "never" form.

This extends the owner's request recorded in [B-PR150-01](B-PR150-01.md) to remove
sentences that disclaim claims a record never made. Label meanings, icons, data
and artwork are unchanged. Merged decision records, the legacy register and
`brand/archive/` keep their wording. The pinned `retro-scraperism` snapshot still
contains the slogan list; the project guide takes precedence, and the matching
change belongs in the owner's Skills workspace before a new pin.

Verification: `python3 scripts/check_brand.py` and the repository unit tests.
