# Commit icon reference drafts from a redistributable model

Partial change to [B-PR102-01](B-PR102-01.md) and [B-PR103-01](B-PR103-01.md);
neither is superseded. Generated Retro-Scraperism drafts that guide the
interface icons may be committed as a study when the model's licence permits
redistribution. Drafts from models without that permission stay unpublished.
Shipped icons remain redrawn SVG; no generated pixels are traced or embedded.

## Changes

- Adds [`brand/studies/icon-drafts/`](../studies/icon-drafts/provenance.json):
  21 drafts, one per `today` icon, from FLUX.2 [klein] 4B with the Qwen3 4B text
  encoder (both Apache-2.0), downscaled to 512 px WebP. Seeds, prompts, source
  and derivative hashes are in the study provenance.
- [Icon provenance](../../site/brand/icons/provenance.json) now points to the
  study. The unpublished 9B round-3 records (FLUX Non-Commercial License) stay
  there for history.
- [`brand/icons.md`](../icons.md) and the manifest state the licence condition.
- The SVG icons are unchanged; the 4B drafts confirm the same compositions.

## Evidence and limits

- User-directed: the owner asked whether 4B quality was sufficient, installed
  the Qwen3 4B encoder on their ComfyUI and approved switching after a 9B/4B
  comparison on the same prompts and seeds.
- Round 5: 21 icons × 3 seeds; `view.table` comes from the round-4 comparison
  run, the only 4B draft with three rows by two columns.
- Known mismatches: `state.pending` drafts put the dots on a ring (the map and
  SVG have none); `view.table` rows are uneven; tick counts vary.
- Originated in [PR #130](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/pull/130).
