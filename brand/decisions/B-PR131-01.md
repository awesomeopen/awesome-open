# Fit specimens to the actual glass interiors

[PR #131](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/pull/131) corrects the
fitting and vertical placement portion of [B-PR127-01](B-PR127-01.md).
Its preparation, deterministic worst-fit-first assignment and selected-project
identity rules remain. The previous implementation fitted historical inset
rectangles correctly, but those rectangles did not match the photographic
inner walls. Passing its containment tests did not establish the intended look.

The owner supplied a specimen-jar photograph as the composition target and a
production screenshot showing small centrally floating drawings. Both were
visually inspected. The target is evidence for sizing and placement only; it
is not copied into the repository or treated as licensed artwork.

Calibrate separate specimen interiors against all seven existing photographs.
Contain each whole silhouette with one movement allowance, then place 90% of
unused vertical room above it and 10% below. The measured bounds belong to
specimen fitting only: glass feathering, crack regions, lights, stickers, cork,
vessel dimensions and movement remain unchanged. The detached lid constrains
the usable right edge of the low-lid jar. Existing opaque stickers remain in
front of the illustration.

The central cylinder is an explicit limitation. Its previous 37–63% window
extended beyond inner walls measured at 40–61% of the source canvas. OpenObserve's
left ribbons are part of its complete silhouette. Preserving them inside the
actual glass reduces its fitted width from approximately 45.3px to 35px, about
23%, rather than filling the tall cylinder. The reference's taller occupancy
cannot be reproduced there without a separate decision about cropping,
proportions, grouping, artwork or vessels. None is proposed here.

Broad-jar placement may change as the deterministic matcher uses the corrected
interior proportions. The saved seven, draw counts, selection probabilities,
cooldown and archive remain unchanged. All asset bytes and provenance are
unchanged, so the asset lock requires no refresh.

Validation requires actual matched before/after stage screenshots from the PR
base and head, plus desktop/mobile, active motion and reduced-motion checks.
Geometric tests cover complete-subject containment, one inset, lower placement,
determinism and invalid-bounds fallback. Neither tests nor this draft record
claim aesthetic approval; the central exception remains for review.
