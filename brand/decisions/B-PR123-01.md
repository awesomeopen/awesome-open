# Broaden the working audience to include curious browsers

The working audience in [Foundation](../foundation.md#purpose-and-scope) was
"people comparing software and following those sources, especially developers
and self-hosters", inferred from the interface. On 2026-10-19 the owner
confirmed it and added a second group: curious browsers who explore the catalog
and its specimen world (artwork, jars, weekly draws, Observatory) without a
specific tool in mind.

The owner also set two current success measures, recorded in the root
[PRODUCT.md](../../PRODUCT.md): fast inspection of a project's function, official
source and evidence, and a specimen experience memorable enough that people
return and share it.

Evidence: owner answers during an Impeccable `init` interview in this PR's
session. Limitations: this is an owner-confirmed framing, not an audience study.
It changes no evidence contract, voice rule or visual rule; the specimen concept
and literal factual labels are unchanged. Design work for browsers must not
trade away inspection for comparers.
