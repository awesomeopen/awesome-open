# Catalog as one screen in three columns

The Catalog fills one desktop screen with three columns and no top or bottom bar:

- **Rail** (Prussian, with the `navypattern2` texture): the botanical mark; All and the
  categories by short name in alphabetical order, with counts that follow search and
  filters; the filters, each select's title merged into its first option
  ("Pushed: any date"); Reset filters; a centred About link.
- **List**: the "Awesome Open*" wordmark and search, then one row per project with
  position, name, HN sparkline and a one-line description cut by an ellipsis. In
  All, the description starts with its first category.
- **Pane**: the jars at rest, or one plate at a time. ← → and ↑ ↓ step through the
  list and wrap; Esc returns to the jars. The plate head shows the project name, the
  stepper, Compare (reading "Added to Cabinet" once added) and close, as plain
  controls without outlines.

About opens in the pane with tabs (riders) for About, Observatory, Scoreboard and
Spotlight. The three field-guide pages load there and hide their own header,
section navigation and footer; the About sheet carries the former Catalog footer:
contribution and repository links, the data method, the pages' data links, other
awesome lists, the source revision and licence, and one footer illustration.

The current category and project are marked by a 2px underline, an accented count
or number, and a notch on the column edge pointing at them from the column they
fill. The open row's focus outline is omitted while those marks show it; other
focus outlines are unchanged.

The specimen cabinet opens from a scarlet corner button reading "n of 6".
WebMCPlet's "Search this list" button keeps the corner and the cabinet stacks
above it ([B-PR304-01](B-PR304-01.md)).

Rationale: owner direction (2026-11-11) asked for desktop density and a one-page
app feel; the owner reviewed a local prototype through 2026-11-15. At 1440 × 900
the list shows 16 rows instead of 5.

This changes parts of [B-PR128-01](B-PR128-01.md) (folded rows give way to list rows),
[B-PR134-01](B-PR134-01.md) (footer links move to About) and the
[route shell](../../site/RETRO-SCRAPERISM.md#route-shell-for-sibling-pages-b42)
(pages keep their shell when opened directly). Phone layout keeps one column and
is not yet designed; plate drawings use the existing 512px files.
