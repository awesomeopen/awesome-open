# Replace interpretative specimen voices with a cloned name voice over an ambient scene

The owner auditioned the offline pilot proposed in B-PR120-01 on 2026-10-18 and
judged it failed. Three local TTS models were tried: Maya1, Qwen3-TTS
VoiceDesign and VoxCPM2. They could be steered in pitch, pace, a closed set of
timbres and accents, but not toward the interpretative, function-derived
character the specimen illustrations carry. Randomized accents were considered
as a substitute and rejected: they avoid caricature only by dropping the
interpretation. Measurements, pinned runtimes and test batches are recorded in
the [voice feature plan](../../site/SPECIMEN-VOICE-PLAN.md#outcome-experiments-failed).

Proposed direction, not started:

- One voice, cloned from a friend's recording with that person's documented,
  informed consent, reads the plain project name.
- A quiet ambient scene beneath it, such as bird song, wind, an office, a factory
  or a train station, is chosen from an interpretation of the tool's vibe. The
  interpretative layer moves from the voice to the setting.

Limitations: no cloning model, scene source or effects pipeline has been
selected or tested. Consent, scene-source licensing and provenance must be
settled before any audio is published. The plan's pronunciation, provenance,
accessible-playback and no-autoplay requirements carry over. This record
supersedes only the per-project interpretative voice direction of B-PR120-01;
that record's status and text are unchanged.
