# Most up to date completeness and recency proposal

[PR #240](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/pull/240) prepares the
requested Scoreboard entry for owner review. The [proposed contract](../../site/SCOREBOARD-COMPLETENESS-PROPOSAL.md)
defines a finite, versioned completeness checklist, a count of up to three recent
activity signals, and the oldest successful verification day across all required
evidence as the tie-break. Equal tuples share competition ranks.

The checklist and source-backed absence/applicability schemas, URL-health
freshness, 90-day window and day-precision comparison await owner review. Sparse
or unsupported evidence blocks qualification; an empty board is valid.
Implementation follows schema review and separate evidence backfill. Existing
six-board snapshots remain frozen and valid; historical captures of the new
metric begin only after implementation.

This documentation proposal preserves the current [Scoreboard contract](../../site/SCOREBOARD.md),
[weekly ranking framework](B-PR227-01.md), [compact rows](B-PR234-01.md) and
[measurement history](B-PR236-01.md). It changes no code, data, artwork, icon,
skill or automation. Validation and exact source revisions are recorded in the PR.
