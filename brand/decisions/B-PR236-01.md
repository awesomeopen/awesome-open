# Integrate measured Scoreboard trends on main

[PR #235](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/pull/235) merged into its
stacked feature branch after that branch's parent PR had already merged to main.
This main-target integration carries its reviewed measurement-history feature
forward, preserving intervening navigation, Spotlight and artwork-policy changes.

The [original decision record](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/blob/ee7bdbc72d9e1c8787909b2865af208ea74f193a/brand/decisions/B-PR235-01.md)
keeps its historical identity and source revision. This separate integration
record does not rename or rewrite it. The current tables, weekly freeze,
metric-specific trophies, optional twelve-month measurement views and compact
all-eligible observations follow that proposal and the [Scoreboard contract](../../site/SCOREBOARD.md).

The compact row treatment of [B-PR234-01](B-PR234-01.md) remains intact. Exact HN
start-inclusive/end-exclusive bounds appear in each HN board's method disclosure,
including when the legacy snapshot has no monthly story counts. Initial gaps
and single observations keep their recorded meanings.

This changes neither collection schedules nor generated evidence by hand.
Publisher-owned future captures extend the genuine partial legacy observation.
Exact-head checks and preview review are recorded in this PR; earlier branch
validation is provenance rather than a claim of main publication.
