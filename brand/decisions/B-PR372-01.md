# Full-box collection chronology control

The user requested a full-box collection replay with no visible heading, caption
or `i`, white text on `var(--muted)`, and a newest-first popup without its opening
explanation/count or bottom download link. This partially changes the presentation
in [B-PR368-01](B-PR368-01.md); its evidence, retention and lazy-delivery rules remain.

One named button spans the box, including blank space. Enter and Space open a
native modal with its title and Close button. Escape closes it and restores focus;
a white inset outline marks keyboard focus. Replay remains silent, pauses during
interaction, and respects reduced motion. Undated observations follow dated rows;
equal timestamps preserve source order. All retained rows remain available.

The [visual rule](../visual-system.md#collection-activity-block) and
[collection contract](../../site/COLLECTION-LOG.md) describe the result. The exact
requested white on muted grey (#465665) measures 7.56:1 contrast. Desktop and
390px mobile previews were inspected; local Chromium checks cover full-box corner
clicks, keyboard opening/closing, return focus, lazy retries, stable ordering and
reduced motion. No assets, evidence, budget or workflow rules change. Existing
strict size failures are reported separately in the PR.
