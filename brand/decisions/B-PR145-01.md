# Retire font comparison and settle on Półtawski

The owner directed removal of the font comparison and other serif families after
settling on Półtawski. This fully retires B-PR135-01 and partially replaces
[B-PR140-01](B-PR140-01.md): its retained selector is removed, while Półtawski
regular 400, genuine bold 700 and Commit Mono remain.

The selector UI, JavaScript/CSS and query-carry behavior are removed. Old serif
query URLs simply use Półtawski. Fraunces and Instrument Serif binaries, metadata
and unused notices are retired, removing 222,356 bytes of binary assets; retained
font bytes and their licenses/provenance are unchanged. Dirty builds remove
obsolete output assets. There are no external font requests or stored preferences.

Hosted and offline/no-JavaScript routes retain readable fallback typography.
The maintained historical Observatory mock rebuild uses current typography;
historical records link to frozen pre-retirement sources rather than losing
their evidence. Generic pinned skills remain untouched.

The cleanup preserves concurrently merged jar callouts and compact plate rules
from PRs 141, 143 and 144. Verification covers two-font loading, real unsynthesized
bold, old URLs, mobile/desktop routes, offline embedding, font failure and stale
output cleanup through the existing test/browser infrastructure.
