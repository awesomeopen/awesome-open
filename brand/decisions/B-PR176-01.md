# Remove the standalone download

Owner-directed on 2026-10-22. The site stops generating `standalone.html`, and
the ledger and record footers drop their links to it ("Download" and "Download
complete catalog"). Offline reading and printing use the static record pages,
`catalog.json` and the evidence downloads.

Rationale and measurements are in the
[removal PRD](../../PRD/2026-10-22_Remove-standalone-download.md): the file was
116 MB of a 256 MB site, needed a second full ledger render and doubled test and
browser-check coverage, and the owner knows of no one who uses it.

## What changes

- `brand/visual-system.md`: the paragraph "The standalone download is a
  motion-free ledger with embedded dependencies" and the standalone mentions in
  the record-shell and closing-mark sections.
- `brand/icons.md` and `site/brand/icons.json`: the `link.download` row loses the
  "Download" example and its `standalone.html` source pointer. The Observatory
  report and CSV downloads keep the icon.
- `brand/assets.md` and `brand/maintenance.md`: the standalone is no longer an
  output mode to embed assets into or to check.
- The standalone clauses in B-PR104-01, B-PR111-01, B-PR112-01, B-PR114-01,
  B-PR128-01, B-PR134-01, B-PR150-01 and B-PR158-01 no longer apply; their other
  rules stay, so the supersession list is empty.

The Observatory's standalone report (`observatory/report.html`) is unchanged.

## Status

Accepted. The implementation PR updates the references above and marks this
