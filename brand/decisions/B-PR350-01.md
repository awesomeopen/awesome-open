# Retain dated star history across month rollover

[PR #350](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/pull/350) keeps validated
last-good star histories visible with their own month range after HN advances to
another window. The chart displays “Stale cached result” above it after a window mismatch
or failed refresh. Its disclosure records the displayed period, successful fetch,
latest attempt and provider source. Unavailable history remains Unknown.

II / Repository record retains the current GitHub star total. III / Observations
& evidence names its separate measurement “GitHub star history”: stars created
in each displayed month and retained at the successful fetch. Unknown months
are never appended as zeros. The count sits above the unbroken month range;
both chart captions reserve two lines to keep the baseline aligned at narrow
plate widths. This refines the chart state described in
[B-PR158-01](B-PR158-01.md); the evidence grid and chart scales stay unchanged.

The [visual system](../visual-system.md) and
[detailed guide](../../site/RETRO-SCRAPERISM.md) describe the display.
[Contributor guidance](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#cached-github-star-history) records the
400-attempt daily budget, 100-repository cap and ten-healthy-day capacity model
for a curated catalog near 1,000 repositories, plus opt-in recovery through the
existing bounded backfill. Tests cover rollover, fresh and stale states, current
totals, daily accounting, quota stops and the 1,000-repository rotation.

The proposal changes no artwork, tokens, credentials, permissions or schedules.
Backfill collection and production publication remain separate operator actions.
