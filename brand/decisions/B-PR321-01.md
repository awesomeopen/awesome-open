# Broader and matched HN evidence series

The compact HN chart shows title/URL search candidates. The plate overlays
narrower scarlet bars for stories matched to the project, on the same months and
shared cube-root scale. Separate labeled counts link to exact generated evidence
lists. All matched stories belong to the broader candidate union.

This extends the sparse-chart scale decision [B-PR319-01](B-PR319-01.md).
Broader candidates express possible matches; strict identity rules still govern
Scoreboard rankings. Legacy matched-only snapshots remain labeled until the
bounded collector completes both series. Candidate-only evidence can display a
chart with zero matched stories. The existing collection budgets, publication
ownership and archive retention stay in place.

The OBS example motivated this distinction: its old repository-only coverage
omitted its own website and named third-party coverage. The reviewed OBS identity
now accepts its official domain, full names, and the short alias with relevant
context. Broader queries use exact-word matching to avoid prefix expansion.

Implementation: [PR #321](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/pull/321).
References: [foundation](../foundation.md), [visual system](../visual-system.md),
[method](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#hacker-news-attention-sparklines).

Validation uses synthetic candidate collisions, strict subsets, archive round
trips, legacy migration, matching rules, responsive DOM and keyboard navigation.
Live candidate coverage rolls out through the existing publisher after review.
