# Launch videos: neutral video layer and labelled content boxes

Launch videos have two visual layers. **Content** is footage: Awesome Open\* pages
and other clients' interfaces such as ChatGPT, shown as they were captured, plus the
catalog value check, which presents catalog records in the site's own style.
**The video layer** is everything the video adds: captions, box labels, the
real-time clock, speed, waiting and cut tags, and the source-tagged claims. It is
neutral (white sans-serif text on near-black chips, `launch-video/src/theme.js`)
and does not use the site's tokens, so viewers can tell what the video says from
what a page shows.

Footage sits in labelled boxes on one neutral dark stage. A box naming its source
("ChatGPT · the owner's Mac", "ChatGPT's cloud browser · Awesome Open\*") stays on
screen while a detail lifts out of it into its own box and returns there when it
leaves, so the viewer keeps track of whose screen they are looking at. Awesome
Open\* boxes carry a heavier white edge; other clients' boxes a thin grey one; the
label text states the source either way.

Slow stretches stay sped up and carry a "Waiting for …" tag rather than being cut
short, so the pace stays followable.

Owner review of the first V1 storyboard (2026-11-16): captions looked like part
of the pages and should be inverted and leave the style guide; zooms should make
clear when the viewer sees Awesome Open\* and when someone's ChatGPT, on a unified
neutral background with the camera moving between content boxes; a waiting tag
beats moving too fast. Scope: `launch-video/` only. No site, artwork, font, icon
or skill-pin change.
