# Discovery catalog review in the workflow diagram

The owner accepted a switch to source-only discovery draft PRs for owner review.
The registered discovery task follows that direction. The
[workflow](../../WORKFLOW.md#flowchart-brief) shows catalog proposals joining at
**Catalog PR → Owner merge → Main source accepted**; discovery uses this path
with a `README.source.md`-only draft PR.

This partially revises the direct-discovery route described in
[B-PR213-01](B-PR213-01.md). Its URL-submission proposal, distinction between
receipt, acceptance and deployment, and enrichment after catalog acceptance
remain unchanged. The original decision and its status stay intact.

Enrichment keeps separate scoped draft PRs. The diagram identifies owner merge
review for summaries and other evidence, and links the existing
[validated new-artwork-only publication procedure](../../site/SPECIMEN-ART.md#publication-after-validation)
for artwork. That procedure's technical gates and authorization are unchanged.

[PR #297](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/pull/297) reconciles the
public guides. This record adds no interface, generated illustration, registered
job, permission or publication path.
