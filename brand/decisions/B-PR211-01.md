# Show every verified HN story history

Display a chart whenever a completed current-window collection contains at least
one verified matching story. Keep zero-story collections without charts. Every
chart links to the date-bounded HN search, including single-story charts, so the
interaction stays consistent.

[PR #211](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/pull/211) implements the
owner-requested display change. The shared square-root scale includes all visible
histories. Identity matching, the 24 completed-month window, collection budgets
and schedules remain unchanged.

Historical `eligible` flags and threshold metadata retain their original meaning
in caches, queue records and immutable observations. Renderers derive current
visibility and scale without rewriting collected evidence. See the
[HN method](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#hacker-news-attention-sparklines).
