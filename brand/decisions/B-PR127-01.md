# Balanced weekly jar fitting with motion clearance

[PR #127](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/pull/127) proposes a focused
refinement of B38's fitting, without changing its seeded selection or cooldown.
The 2026-10-19 edition exposed a flat book in the tallest jar: maximizing the sum
of occupied fractions could sacrifice one window. Assign the same selected seven
by maximizing the least-filled window first, then total fill; project-ID order
continues to break exact ties deterministically.

Aim for 94% subject fill, capped by 4.25 CSS px horizontal and 2.25 CSS px vertical
clearance on each side at the fixed enhanced footprint. These guards cover the
unchanged pointer depth (up to 3px horizontally), idle drift (1px horizontally,
2px vertically) and fractional-pixel rounding. A narrow jar can therefore retain
more than 6% total space; a blanket 94% fit is not safe during motion.

Prepare the three missing OpenTaco, Open Moderator and Open3D transparent
cut-outs from approved originals using the existing recipe. Preserve originals,
older derivatives, source identities and approval status. Missing or malformed
bounds use the full canvas. No runtime pixel processing is introduced.

Preserve all seven photographic vessels and their geometry, glass layers, cork,
stickers, parallax, pause/reduced-motion behavior and third-click inspection.
There are no grouped or repeated specimens. See the [draw contract](../../site/WEEKLY-DRAWS.md)
and [asset provenance](../assets.md#specimen-plate-derivatives).

Validation: whole-selection, reversed-input determinism, actual first-edition,
invalid/missing-bound and motion-envelope regression tests; unchanged existing
browser interaction checks plus exact-head CI screenshot inspection. Visual
acceptance remains pending review; this record does not claim approval to merge.
