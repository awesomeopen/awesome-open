# Launch videos: title cards, numbered highlights and no waiting tag

This changes part of [B-PR343-01](B-PR343-01.md); its neutral video layer and
labelled content boxes stay.

- **No waiting tag.** The centred "Waiting for …" tag is dropped. Sped-up
  stretches are marked by the speed tag and real-time clock in the top right,
  which B-PR343-01 already shows.
- **Title and closing cards.** Each video opens with a 2 s card that is also the
  YouTube still: a full-frame image, a kicker ("WebMCP for Awesome Lists · n of
  3") starting at half height, then a title and a subtitle, left-aligned and
  one line each, in the video layer's sans-serif. The Awesome Open\* fruit
  (`site/brand/fruit-96.png`; in V2 the WebMCPlet lockup) and the Jumper MCP mouse
  sit side by side in the top-right corner, directly on the image; the dark marks
  show reversed in white on the night images. The video closes with a 2 s card on
  a second image, text left-aligned and centred vertically, without marks, that
  fades to black over 1 s. The images are in `launch-video/images/`.
- **Numbered highlights.** Three per video, numbered 1 to 3, mark where to look:
  a red (`#E5322D`) frame or underline with a red numbered disc. The number
  carries the order, so the marker does not depend on colour alone.

Owner review of the V1 to V3 drafts (2026-11-16): waiting tags in the middle of
the screen are not needed; an opening card per video doubles as the YouTube
still; three highlights per video make the flow as easy to follow as 1-2-3.
Scope: `launch-video/` only. No site, artwork, font, icon or skill-pin change.
