# Center departure contours on plain Prussian blue

User-directed implementation for review in [PR #129](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/pull/129),
based on source `8423a7cfbbdd3bacf2510b03b76634c937402823`.

Center the decorative departure SVG around the “In the wild” link: move its
ring center from y=1150 to y=200 in the existing 1000 × 400 viewBox. Preserve
its seeded variation, line styling, aspect-ratio handling and band clipping.
The former center placed nearly all contours outside the visible band.

Remove the repeating navy texture from this band, leaving plain Prussian blue.
Keep the footer texture and its image bytes unchanged; restore the footer-only
role in the asset index and pattern provenance. This partially changes
[B-PR128-01](B-PR128-01.md): its filter-note removal and compact row spacing remain.
B29’s specimen floor and other plate sections are unchanged.

The centered geometry matches the [example Site](https://awesome-open-contour-study.stefan-laetzer.chatgpt.site)
(wrapper commit `5a7135cb2cf0dc125b56e73e0145cb3bdd9d760a`), with its optional
navy texture disabled as requested. Preview controls and example links belong to
the isolated study and are not added to the Catalog.

Validation: Catalog JavaScript tests include a visible-inner-contour regression
check. Brand integrity, whitespace and the focused pattern build test are checked.
Browser visual verification was unavailable in this environment.
