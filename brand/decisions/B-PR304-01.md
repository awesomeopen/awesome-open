# Cabinet as three steps and the WebMCPlet corner button

[PR #304](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/pull/304) changes two Catalog
components; [B-PR250-01](B-PR250-01.md) stays the record of the Cabinet's behavior.

**Specimen cabinet.** The PRD records the owner's review: its interface was uninviting and
hid its purpose. The folded instrument keeps its name, place, rules, table and controls,
and changes their order and framing:

- The Prussian bar with "Experimental / WebMCP" becomes a mist heading: "Specimen
  cabinet", "Compare up to six projects side by side", and "N of 6 selected".
- The body is three numbered steps in reading order: 1 Find, 2 Arrange, 3 Compare, with
  scarlet step numbers. Search came after the selection before; an empty cabinet now
  starts at its first action. The empty state reads "Nothing selected yet. Add projects
  from step 1." Compare is disabled until something is selected.
- Native WebMCP status, the activity log and the method move into "Agents, activity &
  method". Status messages sit above the steps, beside the search they usually report.

**Corner button.** WebMCPlet's "Search this list" button sits at the bottom right and
opens a modal search over the same catalog ([webmcplet/README.md](../../webmcplet/README.md#panel)).
It never opens or moves by itself and has no entry motion. On the Catalog it takes the
tokens through custom properties: Prussian fill and rules, Półtawski headings, Commit
Mono text, a 2px radius and a 3px scarlet focus ring. Visitors can hide it in their
browser. The assistant section stays off on the Catalog: it missed the pass threshold
on the video-comparison journey.

**Evidence.** Desktop (1440px) and 390px captures of both components were inspected, and
`scripts/check_cabinet_browser.cjs` and `scripts/check_webmcplet_browser.cjs` pass
keyboard, focus, touch, dismissal and silencing checks. No artwork, palette or token
value changes. Status stays proposed until the owner reviews the captures.
