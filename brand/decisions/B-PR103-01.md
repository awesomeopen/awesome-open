# First interface icon set and whole-pixel strokes

Adds the 21 `today` icons from the [icon map](../icons.md) as SVG at their listed
sizes (54 files) in `site/brand/icons/`, written by `scripts/build_icons.py`.
The site does not use them yet; wiring them into the ledger and Observatory is a
separate change. Partial change to [B-PR101-01](B-PR101-01.md) and
[B-PR102-01](B-PR102-01.md) (stroke sizes, ticks); neither is superseded.

## Changes to the map

- **Strokes:** 1px at 12, 2px at 16 and 24, replacing 1.5px at 12 and 16. A
  1.5px stroke never lands on whole pixels and rendered soft in review. Accent
  strokes (check, cross, dash, question mark) are 1, 1.5 and 2 px.
- **Registration ticks:** ring and square bases carry four 2px-wide ticks,
  1/1/3 px long at 12/16/24. User-directed after the generated draft rounds and
  kept for review; they crowd the 12px ring.
- **Colour hook:** structure uses `currentColor`; the accent part uses
  `var(--icon-accent, currentColor)` so CSS applies the row's token.

## Evidence

- Style direction: three generated draft rounds on the owner's ComfyUI. The
  owner rejected Speculative FOSSoligy for icons (reserved for specimens) and
  chose Retro-Scraperism only, with ticks. Round 3 picks guided composition.
- The drafts are not published: FLUX.2 [klein] 9B is under the FLUX
  Non-Commercial License and redistribution was not cleared. Seeds, prompts and
  hashes are in [icon provenance](../../site/brand/icons/provenance.json), with
  the icons that were drawn differently from their drafts.
- Reviewed as rendered contact sheets at 1× and 6× zoom on paper, mist and
  Prussian blue with token colours (resvg). No browser, forced-colours or
  in-page check: the icons are not placed in the site yet.

## Limits

- At 12px the ring icons are faint and `state.unknown` is busy at every size.
