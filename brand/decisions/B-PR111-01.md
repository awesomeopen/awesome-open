# Load specimen details when their native drawer opens

The initial catalog retains all summary rows, current visible numbering and the
compact search index. A native drawer requests its complete generated record on
opening. Keep a visible full-record link for no-JavaScript access, failures and
printing. The static record reuses the B42 Observatory shell around the existing
B29 specimen plate. Its evidence, artwork, Folio and Registry identity do not
change. Hash permalinks and QR destinations still point to the catalog.

Keep at most three mounted closed dynamic plates, without evicting an open
record. A reopened evicted plate restores its nested disclosure state. The
complete downloadable standalone and exhibition preview stay fully inlined;
no new service or JavaScript dependency is introduced.

This is a delivery/retention change, not a redesign of the plate, hero, tokens,
artwork, evidence semantics or source ownership. Preserve responsive hero assets,
lazy images, deferred plate effects and offscreen motion pauses. Native keyboard
and focus behavior must survive asynchronous loads and retries.

The live baseline at `775665d7565d84b5bcc0c446ac6839fabcaafb71` had 58,390 elements,
including 51,387 inside closed detail plates. Reducing that structure is the goal;
no specific process-memory reduction is established by those counts.

Implementation, verification and limits: [PR #111](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/pull/111)
and the [loading contract](../../site/DETAIL-LOADING.md). This draft remains
proposed pending review. It partially changes when B29 content is materialized,
not the contents or meanings of B29, B32 or B42.
