# Preserve originals once and verify final artwork by batch

[PR #332](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/pull/332) proposes the
[batch candidate procedure](../../automation/artwork-candidates.md). Owner review
and merge are required before using it.

The proposed batch candidate procedure refines B-PR286-01's finalization and
publication recovery steps. It preserves generation archives, exact prompts,
source evidence and generation receipts under their original plans. Publication
subsets retain the old index and identify other planned projects as deferred.
Prior skips and quarantines retain their meaning and retry restrictions.

Final files are validated locally, committed in one art-only batch and fetched
independently from the remote branch. The durable batch index stores original
references and the remote candidate locator. Metadata corrections repeat local
checks and remote verification without repeating external finalization archives.
All source, pixel, rights, safety, full offline tests, build, browser, exact-head
CI, PR protection and Pages checks remain. New-art-only standing publication
authorization is unchanged. Policy/code remains owner-reviewed and draft.

No accepted artwork, generation policy, pinned or installed skill, workflow,
schedule, credential or security setting changes. This proposal requires owner
review and merge before use. The OpenOCD candidate remains local under the
current contract. Legacy recovery files and receipts remain readable unchanged.
