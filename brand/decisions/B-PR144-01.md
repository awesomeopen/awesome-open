# Compact specimen labels and evidence

User-directed refinement of B29 in [PR #144](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/pull/144).
Illustrated records move the label into the lower illustration by 4em. Negative
margin reduces occupied flow height so the following bands can move up, while a
taller neighboring function or feature column still determines the study height.
Unillustrated records retain a positive margin and stay inside their plate.

The label is 65% wide with a white background, 1px rule border, 2px radius,
0.8 opacity and links without underlines. A 320px minimum, capped to available
width, keeps QR and text usable on narrow screens. The QR has a 2px white border.
This is a plain panel, not a return to B28's retired paper textures.

Evidence has four equal columns above 1100px, two through medium widths, then
one at 720px and below. HN stories, GitHub stars, GitHub release and OpenSSF retain
their source data, order and disclosures. Charts shrink with their columns.

The two supplied screenshots informed placement and column density. The only
adaptation to the requested 65% label width is the narrow-screen minimum above;
normal study height still respects neighboring prose. No artwork bytes changed.
See the [visual system](../visual-system.md) and [guide](../../site/RETRO-SCRAPERISM.md).
Browser geometry assertions cover overlap, equal columns and unillustrated bounds;
local browser rendering was unavailable. This draft awaits visual review.
