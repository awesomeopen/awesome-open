# Private Git retention for artwork originals

Use the private Git transport in [the archive contract](../../automation/artwork-git-archive.md) for the
finite artwork backfill. This partially extends B-PR332-01 and B-PR286-01;
original plan identities, generation receipts, reservations, skips, quarantine,
truthful reviews and all publication gates remain unchanged.

Retain actual originals, prompts and source evidence in a separately verified
private repository. Require immutable commits, SHA-256 checks and independent
remote restores of actual bytes, including receipt/index checkpoints. Preserve
legacy Drive archives and receipts and stop on access failures. The OpenOCD pilot verified a private destination; its locators remain private.
No LFS quota is known. A measured pilot and an owner
capacity review before 750 MB precede wider rollout.

The policy merged in [PR #339](https://github.com/awesomeopen/awesome-open/pull/339)
on 2026-11-16 at `0e82031db665e99973c7b59c61a9c34d39dbbb7e`.

completed recovery and normal publication verification; see the
[archive contract](../../automation/artwork-git-archive.md#verified-openocd-pilot)
for evidence and continuing prerequisites.
