# Private Git retention for artwork originals

Propose the private Git transport in [the archive contract](../../automation/artwork-git-archive.md) for the
finite artwork backfill. This partially extends B-PR332-01 and B-PR286-01;
original plan identities, generation receipts, reservations, skips, quarantine,
truthful reviews and all publication gates remain unchanged.

Retain actual originals, prompts and source evidence in a separately verified
private repository. Require immutable commits, SHA-256 checks and independent
remote restores of actual bytes, including receipt/index checkpoints. Preserve
legacy Drive archives and receipts and stop on access failures. No destination
is currently verified and no LFS quota is known. A measured pilot and an owner
capacity review before 750 MB precede wider rollout.

The owner requested this policy preparation; review and merge are prerequisites
to use. No archive creation/upload, generation, publication, workflow, credential
or protection change is included. Only after those prerequisites may the existing
OpenOCD archive proceed through clean restore, a separate artwork draft PR,
exact-head CI, permitted merge, publisher and Pages verification without regeneration.
