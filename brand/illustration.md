# Illustration

Use the pinned [Speculative FOSSoligy skill](../.agents/skills/speculative-fossoligy/SKILL.md)
for eligible drawings and the [Retro-Scraperism skill](../.agents/skills/retro-scraperism/SKILL.md)
for the project's interface context. The detailed [specimen handoff](../site/SPECIMEN-ART.md)
governs identities, review and publication.

Preserve these project-specific choices:

- One readable subject with one governing impossibility; connected anatomy or
  construction, selective ink/pencil detail and a function-derived analogy.
- White, cool mist, blueprint or transparent grounds override the portable
  drawing skill's warm-white default. Avoid faux aging and paper stains.
- Vary silhouette, view and structure between tools. Machinery is optional;
  do not repeat branches, shells or insect bodies by habit.
- Artwork is original Awesome Open interpretation, not an official project logo.
  Never substitute a specimen for a software project's identity without labeling it.
- Preserve accepted assets during focused edits. A change in star ranking does
  not invalidate existing art.
- Photographic glass vessels and the commissioned panoramic footer backgrounds
  are separate asset families. Their presence does not expand the portable
  FOSSoligy skill's subject scope to landscapes, cities or humans.

## Deliver and review

Save optimized specimen bytes under `site/specimens/` and an entry in
`data/specimen_art.json`, keyed by full canonical catalog ID. Retain actual
dimensions, concise alt text, source-document provenance, prompt and review status.
Only approved existing assets render. Pending or missing art leaves no placeholder.
“Approved” in the manifest means reviewed for inclusion, not a production merge.

Review silhouette, anatomy, functional analogy, palette, background, legibility
at use size, and differentiation from other specimens. Store actual results, not
claims based solely on the prompt. Use the image-generation tool for raster edits.
Refresh the asset checksum inventory after an intentional file change.

Do not bring watermarked inspiration images, third-party book plates, or private
chat screenshots into the public repository. Their stylistic influence is not a
redistribution license. Keep links and rights qualifications in provenance.
