01 / The working principle

Order makes
the strange legible.

This is a reference for the proposed design system, not the redesigned catalog. Start with a useful instrument; introduce a specimen and a small, purposeful editorial interruption.

01

The instrument

A stable grid, readable evidence and controls that explain themselves.

02

The specimen

Observed structure with one coherent impossibility. Pure biology is welcome; gears are optional.

03

The editorial hand

A bracket, underline or note with a referent. Attention, not artificial alarm.

05 / Artwork direction

Strange on second look.
Clear on the first.

Ink, pencil, crosshatching and selective color. Give every specimen a readable silhouette and one governing impossibility. Let its structure explain the invention.

Keep the tension

Technical drawing against organic form. Hard rules against a loose stroke. Deep blue against a flash of jewel color.

Lose the costume

No compulsory brass mechanism, wax-seal button, stained card, torn text edge or photoreal desk. This reference intentionally contains no republished moodboard artwork.

Generate a new specimen when a specific component gives it a job.
